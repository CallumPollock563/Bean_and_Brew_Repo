Nutrition Info

Drinks

Our coffees and drinks can often be customised with different types of milk, syrups, and toppings. Please note that these additions will affect the overall nutritional content, including calories, sugar, and fat levels.


Cakes & Sweet Treats

Our cakes and baked goods are indulgent by nature and may be high in sugar, fat, and calories. They are intended as occasional treats to be enjoyed as part of a balanced diet.


Sandwiches & Savoury Items

Our sandwiches are prepared using fresh ingredients and may include a variety of fillings such as meats, cheeses, vegetables, and sauces. Nutritional values will vary depending on the specific ingredients chosen.
